Connect your TV

  1. Turn the TV on. Make sure it's on the same Wi-Fi as this computer.
  2. Tap Find my TV, or enter its IP address (TV Settings › General › Network › Network Status › IP Settings).
  3. Tap Connect, then choose Allow on the TV screen.

To turn the TV on from this remote, enable Power On with Mobile on the TV (Settings › General › Network › Expert Settings).
